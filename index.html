    Plenty of Fish in the Sea started with a rather school of thought: why does so much of our relationship vocabulary revolve around water?

    We drift apart, give lukewarm answers, get catfished, try to go with the flow, and sometimes end up with someone who's a little too hard-shelled. Apparently, even our love lives have aquatic tendencies.

    And perhaps this was all written in the stars, because I'm a Pisces too. ♓ Maybe I was always destined to make a game about fish and questionable romantic decisions. The universe works in mysterious (and occasionally mildly fishy) ways.

    This little linguistic rabbit hole (or should I say, fishing hole?) collided with a fond memory of an old rotating musical fishing toy I used to play with. And just like that, a childhood memory and an overthinking session had a fishy little love child.

    The result is an interactive satire game that explores how we navigate the dating pool, the red flags we overlook, the patterns we repeat, and the questionable catches we convince ourselves are the one.

    Because, sure, there's plenty of fish in the sea. But how many are actually worth keeping?

    I made this game in the hope that you'll have a little fun, spot a few familiar characters, and maybe reflect on your own fishing habits along the way. No fish were harmed in the making of this game. Egos, perhaps.

    Your turn to take the bait.

    If you played the game, I'd love to know what stood out to you, which fish felt a little too familiar, or what you'd add to this wonderfully questionable ocean.

    And yes, I'm absolutely fishing for compliments. Constructive criticism is welcome too :))

    Thanks for your time!
